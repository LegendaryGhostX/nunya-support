# Support — Nunya

Nunya is a private photo and video vault: Face ID or a passcode gates the
app, and there is no server, no account, no cloud, and no network connections.

## Get help

Email nunya.support@proton.me. This is a dedicated contact channel for Nunya
support requests, not a personal email address and not an issue tracker.

## Frequently asked questions

### I forgot my passcode

There is no way to recover a forgotten passcode. Nunya has no server and no
account, so there is nothing to reset it from — the passcode lives only in
this device's Keychain. If you can't get in with Face ID or your passcode,
the only way forward is to delete and reinstall the app, which removes the
vault, people and plans.

iOS may keep the app's small Keychain records (the passcode hash, the
failed-attempt counter and a legacy device identifier) after the app is
deleted. Nunya overwrites or clears them the next time it is installed and
opened, and nothing else survives.

### What does Wipe this device remove?

"Wipe this device" in Settings deletes vault photos and videos, people,
plans, any chats and messages, settings, and any preserved copies of an
unreadable data file. It also resets the passcode, clears the failed-attempt
counter, deletes the legacy device identifier, and returns the app to
first-launch setup. The optional "Erase after 10 failed attempts" setting
performs the same wipe. It counts wrong passcodes entered on the passcode
screen only; guesses typed on the calculator decoy never trigger it and never
add a delay.

Deleting the app removes all of its files. iOS may keep the app's small
Keychain records (the passcode hash, the failed-attempt counter and a legacy
device identifier) after the app is deleted. Nunya overwrites or clears them
the next time it is installed and opened, and nothing else survives.

### Does Nunya use the internet or nearby devices?

No. This version of Nunya makes no network connections of any kind. It does
not use the local network or Bluetooth, and it advertises nothing to nearby
devices.

Nearby phone-to-phone messaging is planned for a future update. The privacy
policy will be updated before it ships.
